# Code of conduct

Jade adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project, in every project
space: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any other channel where someone acts on
behalf of the project.

## Reporting

Report behavior that breaks the code of conduct by email to the maintainer at
<aerafal.github@gmail.com>. Only the maintainer reads these reports, and they are kept
confidential. Links or screenshots help when you can include them.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report, may ask the people involved for more information, and decides
on a response following the Contributor Covenant's guidance on addressing and repairing harm, from
a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project spaces.
